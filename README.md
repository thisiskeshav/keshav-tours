# Keshav Tours and Travels — Himalayan Holiday Portal

A modern, fully responsive, and interactive business website for **Keshav Tours and Travels**, specializing in mountain tours and travel packages across **Himachal Pradesh**, **Kashmir**, and **Leh Ladakh**.

Built with **React 19**, **Vite 7**, and **Tailwind CSS**, modeled closely after the premium travel agency visual reference.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Hero & Trust Assurance
- **Cinematic Himalayan Panorama**: Dramatic high-resolution mountain landscapes with gradient overlays.
- **Dynamic Eyebrow**: *"Your Himalayan Dream Vacation Awaits | Himachal • Kashmir"*.
- **Quick Action CTAs**: "Explore Packages" (smooth scroll) and "Watch Our Story" (interactive showcase modal).
- **5 Pillars of Trust Strip**: Safe & Secure Travel, 24/7 Local Kashmiri & Himachali Support, Expertly Curated Itineraries, Best Price Guarantee, and 18,500+ Happy Travelers.

### 2. Live Quick Search & Filter Bar
- Floating glassmorphism search widget directly beneath the hero.
- Reactive filters by **Destination / State** (Himachal, Kashmir, All), **Duration** (Weekend 4-5D, Week 6-7D, Grand 8+D), and **Travel Style** (Honeymoon, Family, Adventure & Snow).
- Real-time matching counter with instant reset.

### 3. Top Mountain Destinations (Reference Card Grid)
- High-impact destination cards featuring:
  - **Manali** (Valley of Gods, Snow & Solang)
  - **Srinagar & Dal Lake** (Paradise on Earth & Houseboats)
  - **Gulmarg** (Asia's Ski Capital & Highest Gondola)
  - **Shimla & Kufri** (Queen of Hills & Heritage Ridge)
  - **Pahalgam** (Valley of Shepherds & Betaab Valley)
  - **Dharamshala & McLeodganj** (Little Lhasa & Dhauladhar)
  - **Spiti Valley** (The Middle Land & Ancient Monasteries)
  - **Sonamarg** (Meadow of Gold & Thajiwas Glacier)
- **Interactive Destination Modal**: View altitude, best season, top attractions, starting price, and filtered packages.

### 4. About Keshav Tours & Travels
- Two-column split layout with framed traveler photo and floating credential badges (12+ Years Experience, Govt. Verified Tourism Partner).
- 4 Core Highlights:
  - Customized Travel Packages
  - Professional Mountain Drivers & Local Guides
  - Transparent All-Inclusive Rates with No Hidden Charges
  - Dedicated Pure Veg & Jain Meal Kitchens

### 5. Travel Services We Offer (Dark Midnight Navy Section)
- 6 Dark-themed service cards with glowing hover effects:
  - Cab & Volvo Fleet Bookings (Innova Crysta, Ertiga, Tempo)
  - Hotels & Heritage Dal Lake Houseboats
  - Customized Holiday Packages (Honeymoon, Family, Senior Citizens)
  - Rohtang Permits & Gulmarg Gondola Passes
  - 4x4 & Royal Enfield High-Altitude Expeditions
  - 24/7 On-Trip Emergency & Assistance Team

### 6. Handcrafted Tour Packages & Itineraries
- Filter pills: All, Himachal, Kashmir, Honeymoon Specials, Family Leisure, Adventure & Snow.
- Rich Package Cards:
  - Discount badges (up to 30% off)
  - Duration, route breakdown, and star ratings
  - Key inclusions checklist (Hotel, Cab, Meals, Sightseeing)
  - Dual action buttons: **View Itinerary** & **Book / Reserve**.
- **Interactive Itinerary Modal**:
  - Full day-by-day itinerary breakdown
  - Inclusions and exclusions side-by-side
  - Verified mountain guarantees
  - Direct 1-click proceed to booking.

### 7. Interactive Himalayan Trip Cost Calculator & Custom Builder
- Choose State (Himachal, Kashmir, Both)
- Duration slider (4, 6, 8, 10 Days)
- Accommodation Standard (Standard 3★, Deluxe 4★, Luxury 5★ Resort & Houseboat)
- Guest counter (Adults & Children)
- Dedicated Vehicle (Sedan, Innova Crysta, Tempo Traveller)
- Add-on toggles (Honeymoon bed decor, Gondola passes)
- **Live Dynamic Price Calculation**: Updates instantly as options change.
- Instant "Reserve Custom Itinerary" & "Send to WhatsApp for Custom Quote" actions.

### 8. Full Booking & Reservation System (with Printable Voucher)
- Complete reservation modal capturing traveler name, WhatsApp number, email, pickup city, travel date, guests, dietary preferences (Pure Veg, Jain food, Non-Veg), and special requests.
- Transparent 25% token advance calculation with balance payable upon hotel arrival.
- **Generates an Official Booking Voucher** with a unique reference number (`KT-XXXXXX`).
- 1-Click **"Send Voucher to WhatsApp"** and **"Print Voucher"** functionality.

### 9. Testimonials & Social Proof
- Verified review cards from guests across Delhi, Pune, Kolkata, and Ahmedabad.
- Star ratings, genuine mountain stories, and TripAdvisor / Google reviews badge.

### 10. Photo Gallery & Lightbox
- High-resolution photographs categorized by region (Himachal & Kashmir).
- Interactive Lightbox modal to view moments in full screen.

### 11. Conversion Banners, Contact & Floating Actions
- "Ready For Your Next Adventure?" panoramic CTA banner.
- Quick callback inquiry form + FAQ accordion answering common travel queries.
- Office addresses in **Shimla**, **Srinagar**, and **New Delhi**.
- Persistent floating **WhatsApp Quick Chat** with notification ping, **Quick Call** button, and **Back-to-top** button.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Start Development Server
Double-click `dev.bat` or run:
```powershell
.\dev.bat
```
Visit: [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173)

### Build for Production
Double-click `build.bat` or run:
```powershell
.\build.bat
```

### Preview Production Build
Double-click `preview.bat` or run:
```powershell
.\preview.bat
```
